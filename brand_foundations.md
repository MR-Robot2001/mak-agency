## 5. Visual Direction: The Modern Indian Heritage
**Concept:** "The Gilded Vault"

*   **Color Palette:** Obsidian (#0A0A0A), Charcoal (#1A1A1A), and **Heritage Gold** (#C5A059). The gold is used as a precision accent, signifying wealth and prosperity without the "hype."
*   **Typography:** *Inter* paired with a clean, high-contrast serif for section titles to reflect a blend of modern tech and deep-rooted heritage.
*   **Imagery:** Subtle geometric patterns inspired by modern Jali architecture, used as low-opacity backgrounds. Focus on imagery that evokes "Scale"—infrastructure, metropolitan skylines (Mumbai/Bangalore), and high-end materials like marble and brass.
